Industry Talk

Grid Stability in the Energy Transition: The Emerging Role of Synchronous Condensers

Abstract

The rapid integration of renewable energy sources and inverter-based resources is transforming modern power systems, creating new challenges in grid stability, voltage regulation, and frequency control. This industry talk explores the emerging role of synchronous condensers in providing reactive power support, physical inertia, and short-circuit strength to enhance power system resilience. Through international case studies and practical industrial experience, the session compares synchronous condensers with other grid support technologies such as STATCOMs and hybrid solutions, offering valuable insights for utilities, renewable energy developers, system operators, researchers, and students working towards a stable and reliable energy transition.

Invited Talk

AI-Assisted Predictive Maintenance for Steam Turbines in Sub-Saharan Africa: Field Insights from a 220 MW Steam Turbine Generator Overhaul

Abstract

This invited talk explores the application of AI-assisted predictive maintenance in steam turbine operations through real-world insights from a 220 MW turbine generator overhaul in Sub-Saharan Africa. It highlights the use of predictive analytics, condition monitoring, and intelligent maintenance strategies to improve power plant reliability, operational efficiency, and asset performance.

Invited Talk

Advanced Optical Concentrators for Building-Integrated Photovoltaic Systems: Design, Optimization and Energy Performance

Abstract

This invited talk explores the use of advanced optical concentrators to improve the efficiency and sustainability of Building-Integrated Photovoltaic (BIPV) systems. It highlights their design, optimization, and integration into solar windows, façades, and skylights, demonstrating their potential to enhance energy performance while reducing photovoltaic material usage.
